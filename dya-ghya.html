1Executive Summary

Dya-Ghya is a two-sided marketplace that enables owners or authorized providers to monetize underused resources by listing them for short- or longer-duration use. Customers discover, compare, book and pay through the platform. The first release focuses on rooms and private/authorized parking, with storage as the next category.

Launch context: the Nashik–Trimbakeshwar Simhastha Kumbh 2027 is the initial high-demand launch opportunity, but the architecture must remain city-agnostic and must not hard-code Kumbh rules, locations or dates.

2Product Vision

Core promise: Have something unused? List it. Let others use it. Earn from it.

  • Permanent brand: Dya-Ghya.
  • Brand line: द्या • घ्या • कमवा / Give • Take • Earn.
  • The platform is a marketplace, not the owner/operator of every listed space.
  • Prioritize lawful use, verification, transparency, evidence and dispute resolution.
  • Must scale beyond Nashik and beyond the 2027 Kumbh.

5Customer Application Requirements

5.1 Authentication & Profile

  • Mobile OTP login.
  • Profile with minimum necessary personal information.
  • Identity/age verification before check-in where required by policy or law.
  • Consent and privacy records.
  • Account security, session management and logout from other sessions where appropriate.

5.2 Home & Search

  • Location/city/area selection.
  • Category selection: Rooms, Parking; future categories must be configurable.
  • Date, start time and end time.
  • Duration: hourly, half-day, full-day, overnight, multi-day, weekly/monthly where supported.
  • Guest count for rooms and vehicle type/count for parking.
  • Map and list views.
  • Sort by relevance, price, distance, rating and availability.

5.3 Filters

Price rangeDistanceAvailability CapacityAmenitiesCovered/open parking Vehicle typeFood preferencesHouse rules Verification badgesCancellation policyAccessibility

5.4 Listing Details

  • Photos and listing description.
  • Location and approximate/map presentation as appropriate.
  • Verification badges with separate meanings.
  • Amenities and capacity.
  • Price and all applicable charges shown before payment.
  • Availability and booking duration.
  • House rules and cancellation policy.
  • Host information limited to what is necessary and permitted.
  • Report listing function.

5.5 Room Requirements

  • Room/property type; maximum occupancy; bed/bathroom information.
  • AC / fan / Wi-Fi / hot water / bedding / drinking water / parking / power backup and other configurable amenities.
  • Food: vegetarian, non-vegetarian, Jain, vegan, both, no food; breakfast/lunch/dinner; outside food; cooking where applicable.
  • Smoking, pets, visitors, quiet hours and other house rules.
Alcohol: MVP status is Not permitted. Any future alcohol-related functionality requires specific legal/excise review.

5.6 Parking Requirements

  • Private or properly authorized parking only.
  • Car/bike/other permitted vehicle type; covered/open; vehicle dimensions and capacity.
  • Entry/exit timings; lighting/security/CCTV if applicable.
  • Hourly/daily/multi-day pricing.
Do not permit public roads, footpaths, no-parking zones, fire access or unauthorized land/common spaces.

5.7 Booking & Payment

  • Booking ID generated for every booking.
  • Availability locked during checkout to prevent double booking.
  • Customer must see price, taxes/fees, cancellation terms and key house rules before payment.
  • Payment gateway integration; card/payment credentials must not be stored by Dya-Ghya.
  • Store gateway transaction reference, amount, status and timestamp.
  • Booking confirmation and receipt/statement.
  • Off-platform payment discouraged/prohibited for platform bookings.

6Host/Owner Application Requirements

6.1 Host Onboarding

  • Mobile OTP; identity verification; contact information.
  • Bank/payout information; applicable tax information.
  • Compliance declarations; acceptance of Host Agreement and platform policies.

6.2 Property/Resource Verification

  • Ownership proof or lawful authorization evidence; relevant property documents.
  • Accommodation registration information where applicable.
  • Photographs; address/location evidence; safety and capacity information.
  • Document expiry tracking.
  • Admin review and approval before listing becomes bookable.

6.3 Verification Badges

Identity VerifiedOwnership/Authorization Verified Property Documents ReviewedAccommodation Registration Verified* Platform Safety Check
Do not use a misleading "100% Verified" badge.

6.4 Listing Workflow

Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Verified/Approved → Live

Other controlled states: Rejected, Expired, Suspended. Only Live listings may receive bookings.

6.5 Host Pricing & Availability

  • Hourly, half-day, daily, overnight, multi-day and weekly/monthly pricing where appropriate.
  • Availability calendar; blackout dates/times; minimum/maximum duration; capacity.
  • Configurable event/seasonal pricing; admin-configurable commission.
  • No automatic price changes without host control.

8Admin & Management Requirements

8.1 Executive Dashboard

  • Customers, hosts and active listings; bookings and GMV.
  • Dya-Ghya revenue/commission; host payouts; refunds.
  • Operating expenses entered/configured for management reporting; net operating contribution.
  • Cancellation and complaint rates; repeat customers; verification backlog.

8.2 Financial Reports

  • Daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual and custom date reports.
  • Gross Booking Value (GMV); platform commission revenue; service/convenience fees where applicable.
  • Payment gateway charges; refunds; host payouts; other recorded operating costs.
  • Net platform revenue/contribution.
  • Reconciliation between booking, payment, refund, payout and ledger records.

8.3 Commission Reports

  • Commission by category, city, host, campaign and event period.
  • Commission rate must be configurable.
  • Historical bookings retain the commission rule applicable at booking time.

8.4 Payout Reports

  • Pending, processing, successful, failed and on-hold payouts.
  • Host, booking, amount, payout date and transaction reference.
  • Payout hold for active disputes according to published policy.

8.5 Refund Reports

  • Booking ID, customer, host, original amount, refund amount, reason, cancellation actor, policy applied and refund status.
  • Refund audit trail.

8.6 Host / Customer / Location / Category Reports

  • Host performance: bookings, revenue, utilization, rating, cancellations and complaints.
  • Customer behavior: new/repeat customers, booking value, cancellation and complaint trends.
  • Location performance: listings, bookings, GMV, revenue, price, utilization and complaints.
  • Category performance: Rooms, Parking, Storage and future categories.

8.7 Kumbh 2027 Command Center

  • Rooms and parking inventory; demand by date and location.
  • Bookings and revenue; average price; booking lead time; peak dates.
  • Cancellation and complaint trends; host availability.
  • Configurable event rules with source, effective dates and last verification.
Do not hard-code official Kumbh rules.

12Security Requirements

  • HTTPS everywhere; role-based access control; server-side authorization for every sensitive resource.
  • Input validation and output encoding; rate limiting and abuse protection.
  • Secure authentication/session handling; encryption where appropriate.
  • Secure file uploads with file-type validation and malware scanning where appropriate.
  • Secrets management — no API secrets in frontend code.
  • Audit logging of sensitive administrative actions.
  • Database backups and tested restore process; monitoring and error logging.
  • Protection against common web vulnerabilities.
  • Payment card data must not be stored by Dya-Ghya.

13Privacy & Data Governance

  • Data minimization; clear privacy notice and consent records where required; purpose limitation.
  • Retention and deletion policy; appropriate data access/deletion workflow.
  • Do not unnecessarily retain raw Aadhaar/identity-document images.
  • Restrict access to sensitive documents using role-based controls.
  • Maintain data-processing and security audit records.

15Reviews & Reputation

  • Only completed bookings can create reviews.
  • Customer reviews may cover accuracy, cleanliness, experience, host behavior and space condition.
  • Host may rate customer according to published policy.
  • Moderation and abuse-reporting workflow.
  • Review history should be linked to booking records.

16Document & Compliance Expiry

  • 30-day reminder before expiry; 7-day warning.
  • Expired document → listing automatically paused where required.
  • Updated document → admin review → listing restoration.
  • Keep document status and expiry history.

17Financial Ledger & Reconciliation

A double-entry-style or otherwise auditable transaction ledger is strongly recommended so every financial movement can be reconciled.

  • Unique transaction ID; booking ID; user/host.
  • Transaction type; amount; timestamp; status.
  • Related refund/payout; actor and reason for manual adjustment.
  • Daily reconciliation between gateway, bookings, refunds, payouts and ledger.

20MVP Acceptance Scope

  1. Customer can register/login and search rooms and parking.
  2. Customer can filter by date, duration, location and relevant attributes.
  3. Customer can view only approved/live listings.
  4. Customer can see price and important rules before payment.
  5. Customer can complete a booking and payment.
  6. Customer can view booking history and cancellation/refund status.
  7. Host can register and complete verification workflow.
  8. Host can create room/parking listings and submit them for approval.
  9. Admin can approve/reject/suspend listings and hosts.
  10. Availability prevents double booking.
  11. Host can view booking-level earnings and payout status.
  12. Admin can view financial ledger, payments, refunds, payouts and commission.
  13. Admin can generate daily/monthly financial and management reports.
  14. Complaint/reporting and suspension workflow is operational.
  15. Audit logs exist for sensitive actions.
  16. Security, privacy and backup controls are tested before live launch.

22Claude Development Rules

  • Build one module at a time; do not generate the entire application in one response.
  • Before coding each module, define requirements, data model, API contracts, validation rules and acceptance criteria.
  • Never invent legal/regulatory requirements; mark assumptions and request professional confirmation where necessary.
  • Use migrations for database changes; use server-side authorization for all protected resources.
  • Write tests for critical booking, payment, payout, refund and permission logic.
  • Keep integrations behind replaceable service interfaces; never expose secrets.
  • Use consistent IDs, timestamps, statuses and audit fields.
  • Document every API and important business rule.
  • Do not change confirmed-booking rules retrospectively without a controlled versioning approach.
  • Keep commission, tax configuration, cancellation policies and event rules configurable.

25Final Product Definition

Dya-Ghya is a verified marketplace for monetizing underused resources. The MVP connects customers with verified hosts offering rooms and private/authorized parking. Customers receive transparent pricing, booking and support; hosts receive a transparent earnings and payout system; administrators receive operational, financial, safety and management controls. Storage and additional asset categories can be added through the same configurable architecture.
